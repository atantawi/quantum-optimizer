Q10 diagnosis: the inner split optimizes the SPLIT, not the station's SHARE
======================================================================================
  classical_dominant at C = 41.0400. Q3 showed r* = 1.0000 at the converged point, so the
  optimal-split policy and the paper choose the IDENTICAL split there. The only
  remaining difference is alloc_cost, hence the SHARE of C the station gets.

  policy    alloc_cost  S(fj_pp)    spend       m1       m2    T(fj)        obj
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  qopt           5.000    1.9062   9.5308   1.9062   7.6247  0.70938   3.463677
  paper          2.000    3.7466   7.4932   3.7466   3.7466  0.45046   2.613335
  split          5.000    1.5920   7.9601   3.9801   3.9801  0.42092   2.620524

  The split policy reports S = spend/(c1+c2) while its true m1 comes from the
  inner solve, so 'S' no longer means 'server 1 runs at S*mu' and eq 22's
  surrogate zeta = T*(S*mu - gamma) is anchored to a rate the station does not
  have. eq 21 then mis-prices the station: here it OVER-funds it (spend 7.96 vs
  the paper's 7.49) and buys a better T(fj) that is not worth what the other
  13 stations gave up for it. Direction is incidental; consistency is the point.
  That mispricing is the whole gap -- the split itself is identical.
